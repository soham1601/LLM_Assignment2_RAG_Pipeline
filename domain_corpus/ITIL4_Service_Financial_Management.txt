Membership Benefits OVERVIEW Previous Next BETTY BEE: FIND KNOWLEDGE FASTER We are pleased to announce Betty Bee, itSMF UK’s AI-powered knowledge assistant – designed to make it simple for members to access useful guidance, next-step thinking, service management resources and insight.Built on trusted itSMF UK content, what sets Betty Bee apart is that the knowledge is rooted in real-world member case study content spanning a variety of industry sectors and organisation sizes.Whether you’re looking for service management guidance, professional development resources, event information, or answers to common questions, Betty Bee can help you find what you need faster.Members can access Betty Bee through the Member Area, while non-members can explore its capabilities via a Sneak Peek version. A dedicated Conference View is also available to help attendees make the most of ITSM26. More about Betty Bee ITSM26: ANNUAL CONFERENCE With more than 50 presentations from across the industry, ITSM26 on 9th-10th November will offer unparalleled service management content and networking.Following an amazing ITSM25, we are excited to announce we’ll be back at Stadium MK in Milton Keynes this year. The event will be all about networking and interaction, from the welcome reception on Sunday evening to the many breakout options and discussions, and of course our Professional Service Management Awards dinner on Monday.The call for speakers has now closed, and we’re reviewing all the topics proposed for this year’s agenda – subjects like AI & ITSM; cyber resilience; customer experience; ITIL Version 5; sustainability; service integration; enterprise service management; service design; change, problem, and major incident management; and humanising ITSM. Keep an eye out for more details! More about ITSM26 PSMA26: ANNUAL AWARDS The Professional Service Management Awards (PSMA) provide a great opportunity to highlight the achievements of IT service management individuals and teams, whose skills, commitment and imagination have marked them out for special recognition. Nominations have now closed for this year’s Team of the Year, Service Innovation, Customer Experience, Young Professional, Contributor of the Year and new ITIL Project awards – more information here. Why not check out the Roll of Honour listing our past winners. MANAGING YOUR SKILLS The Professional Skills Management Framework (PSMF), based on SFIA8, is a practical competency management platform that helps members to build a practical view of their own skills and capabilities. The latest version, based on SFIA 8, includes a whole range of tools for self-assessment, skill gap analysis, and profile building, all for free. Why not give it a go?
